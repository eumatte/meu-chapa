This image is for "Meu Chapa" — a Brazilian streetwear brand from 
Rio de Janeiro. Skate culture, raw street identity, carioca roots.

STYLE RULES (never break these):
- Photography style: editorial product, clean and intentional
- Background for props: pure white (#FFFFFF), no gradients, no texture
- Background for lifestyle: deep red studio (#8B0000) OR Rio de Janeiro streets
- Lighting: soft diffused studio light, no harsh shadows, no lens flare
- Film look: 35mm grain, Kodak Portra 400 or Kodak Gold 200 color grade
- Color palette: black #0D0D0D, cream/off-white #F2EDD7, lime #A8D240, red #9B0000
- Never add logos or branding that does not exist on the actual product
- Never change the colorway of any product (always black unless stated)
- Never use generic stock photo aesthetics — always raw and editorial

GRAPHIC IDENTITY (applies to all printed pieces):
- Logo text: "Meu Chapa" — retro cursive script, cream/off-white color
- Back graphic: vintage sardine can with mermaid woman inside, decorative 
  sparkle stars, tattoo flash art style, cream/off-white ink on black fabric
- Secondary mark: "MC" — bold block letters, always black on white or white on black
- "MEUCHAPA" wordmark: all caps, clean sans-serif, small scale embroidery
